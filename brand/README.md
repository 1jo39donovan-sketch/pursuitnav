# BlueRoute brand

- `logo.svg`: the logo on its navy square (app icon, website, stores). `logo-1024.png` is the same at 1024 × 1024.
- `logo-mark.svg`: the mark alone, transparent, for putting on the navy background.
- Name: **BlueRoute**, one word. In the wordmark "Blue" is light and "Route" blue.
- Font: Inter (bold for the wordmark).
- Colours: navy `#0c1220` (background), amber `#ffb627` (the route), blue `#3d8bff` (the start, "Route"), white `#ffffff` (the arrow), light text `#e8edf6`.
- Wording: blue-light navigation for emergency services (police, ambulance and fire). Don't tie it to one service or to London: both will widen.

Made from these: `app/assets/icon.png` and the Android icons, `store/play-icon.png`, `store/feature-graphic.png` (Google Play banner), `site/share.png` (the card when the website is shared), and the logo on the server's Privacy and Support pages (`server/src/pages.ts`).
