BlueRoute

Get to the call quickly and safely.

Blue-light navigation built for emergency services: police, ambulance and fire. Type the address the way control gives it, compare a standard and a blue-light route, and follow big, glanceable directions.

__STORES__

Free. No account, no adverts, no tracking. For iPhone and Android.

Search the way control gives it

Roads, “54 Caledonian Rd”, flats, postcodes, N7, estates and blocks by name. Misspellings and nicknames forgiven. Your boroughs first.

Two routes, your choice

A legal route, and a blue-light route using bus lanes and gates, never the wrong way down a one-way street, listing every restriction it uses.

Turn-by-turn

Large directions for the operator, re-routing when you leave the route, live TfL closures avoided. Or hand over to Google Maps or Waze.

Pursuit screen

Speed, the road's limit (or “—”, never a guess), road, heading, borough, next junction and where you're heading, every second.

Works with poor signal

Road and place search, the offline map and directions on the current route carry on with no signal.

Private by design

Addresses and your log stay on your phone. The server works out routes without keeping anything.
